Architecture

Built on
Modern Steel.

Spotlight-Win isn't another bloated Electron wrapper. It's engineered with a low-level Rust backend and a lightweight Tauri webview frontend — your system resources belong to your work, not to your search bar.

Powered by the bare-metal Tantivy search library, we deliver full-text indexing and fuzzy matching that outpaces native Windows Search by an order of magnitude.

Every architectural decision was made to eliminate waste: no Node.js runtime, no Chromium overhead, no cloud dependency. Just a single lean binary that does one thing perfectly.
